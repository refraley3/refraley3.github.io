Asclepias tuberosa

Butterfly Milkweed brings an unusually vivid orange to dry summer meadows and prairie openings. Dense flower clusters rise above narrow green leaves, making the plant conspicuous even among taller grasses and surrounding vegetation. The flowers also attract a wide range of insects, turning a botanical portrait into an opportunity to photograph the relationships around the plant.

For identification, habitat, and natural-history details, see the Asclepias tuberosa species page in the Field Notes section. For photographic observations and visual context, see Photographic Sequence in the Field Notes section.
