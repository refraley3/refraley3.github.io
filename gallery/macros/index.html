At close range, the familiar dissolves into pattern and structure. Macro photography reveals the overlooked — droplets, spores, textures, and the quiet geometry of living surfaces — where scale shifts perception, and small details become landscapes of their own. Whether examining spring buds, insects, lichens, or droplets after a rain, these close views bring forward details that often go unnoticed by the naked eye. For additional photographic observations and supporting views, see Photographic Sequence in the Field Notes section.
