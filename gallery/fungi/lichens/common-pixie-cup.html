Cladonia pyxidata — probable identification

For identification, habitat, and natural-history details, see the Cladonia pyxidata page in the Field Notes section. For photographic observations connecting these two views of the colony, see Photographic Sequence in the Field Notes section.

These lichens are presented as Common Pixie-cup, provisionally identified as Cladonia pyxidata. The rough, granular cup-bearing stalks and the coarse-looking granules reported in the microscope view of a cup interior favor that interpretation, but the photographs do not establish a definitive species identification. Cladonia chlorophaea remains an alternative.

A Closer View

Common Pixie-cup — A Closer View
Pale cups rise above a mosaic of small scales, mosses, and plant litter; the granular surfaces give each stalk a textured outline.

The closer composition brings the cup-bearing stalks, or podetia, into prominence. Their uneven rims and pale, granular surfaces stand out against the softer greens and browns behind them. Cups of different heights create a small woodland-like scene, while the mosses, leaf fragments, and crossing stems retain the scale and complexity of the colony's surroundings.

This supporting image places the lichens among the low vegetation growing around them. Broad leaves frame the cups, and scattered stems cross the ground between the clusters. The background of E22B1813-26 is the area featured in the E22B1802-12 close-up above: the two photographs show connected parts of the same colony, with the hero image drawing attention to a group that recedes into the background of this wider view.
