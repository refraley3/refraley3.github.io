Buteo platypterus

The Broad-winged Hawk is a compact woodland raptor, often recognized by its sturdy buteo shape, barred underparts, and relatively short tail. Seen high in bare branches, it can appear quiet and watchful one moment, then suddenly become animated as it calls or shifts position above the open edge.

For identification details and comparison with similar species, see the Buteo platypterus in the Field Notes section.

Overview

This sequence presents a stronger and closer Broad-winged Hawk encounter than the earlier distant flight record. Instead of relying on a small bird against a pale sky, these photographs show the hawk perched in good light, with facial detail, feather pattern, posture, and behavior all visible.

The bare branches frame the bird naturally. A fully cleaned background would make the portrait simpler, but keeping the branch structure preserves the feeling of a real woodland-edge perch.

Identification Notes

In these closer views, the bird shows the compact buteo structure expected of a Broad-winged Hawk: a sturdy body, relatively short tail, rounded head, and folded wings that do not create the long, angular impression of an Osprey. The underparts are marked, the bill is short and hooked, and the yellow cere is visible in the portrait view.

The perched posture also helps separate this bird from an Osprey. An Osprey would usually appear longer-winged, paler below, and more angular overall, with a stronger dark eye stripe and a different pattern through the head and body.

Photographer’s Perspective

The newer photographs turn this from a documentary raptor record into a stronger gallery page. The first image gives a clean identification portrait, while the calling frames add behavior and presence. Leaving some of the surrounding branches in place keeps the sequence natural, especially for a woodland hawk using a bare tree as a high lookout.

Together, the images show both stillness and voice: the hawk first as a perched observer, then as an active presence calling from the branches.
