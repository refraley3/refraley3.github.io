Interrupted Breakfast
A Turkey Vulture pauses after being disturbed from feeding nearby.
Perched on a broad branch among early spring buds, the bird turns its red head toward the interruption. The close view shows the pale bill, bare facial skin, and dark layered plumage that are easy to miss when the species is only seen circling overhead.
