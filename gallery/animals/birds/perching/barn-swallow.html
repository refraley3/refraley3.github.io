Hirundo rustica

Barn Swallows are built around flight, with long pointed wings, streamlined bodies, and deeply forked tails. In this juvenile, the shorter tail streamers, warm buff underparts, and yellow gape give the familiar swallow shape a softer appearance. Brief movements on the perch reveal details that are difficult to see when the bird is moving rapidly through the air.

For identification details and comparison with similar species, see the Hirundo rustica page in the Field Notes section. For photographic observations and visual context, see Photographic Sequence in the Field Notes section.
