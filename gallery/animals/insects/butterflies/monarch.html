Danaus plexippus

The Monarch is one of the most recognizable North American butterflies, with broad orange wings, bold black veins, and black borders marked with white spots. These photos show the butterfly visiting Butterfly Milkweed, nectaring among tall prairie flowers, approaching purple thistle in flight, and as a striped caterpillar on milkweed.

For identification details, life-cycle notes, host plant information, and comparison with similar orange-and-black butterflies, see Danaus plexippus in the Field Notes section.
