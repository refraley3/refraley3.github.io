Bombus griseocollis

The Brown-belted Bumble Bee is a robust native bumble bee with dense golden-yellow and black hair and a characteristic warm brown band near the front of the abdomen. These photographs show the bee from several useful angles: feeding among yellow disk florets, viewed from the side and front, and resting among foliage.

For identification details and field characteristics, see Bombus griseocollis in the Field Notes section.
