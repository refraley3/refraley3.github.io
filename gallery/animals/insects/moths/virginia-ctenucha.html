Feathered Antennae

Feathered Antennae
Comb-like antennae rise above an orange collar and metallic-blue thorax as the dark wings open behind them.
E21A1661 • Size: 1300 x 867

The Virginia Ctenucha is already striking in profile, but this close view brings its most intricate feature forward. The finely branched antennae rise above the head, while the orange collar and blue scales glow against the nearly black wings. A clean foreground lets the moth hold the frame without losing its setting among the grass.

Wings Across the Grass

Wings Across the Grass
Broad, pale-edged wings sweep through the wet vegetation as orange and cobalt markings emerge beneath them.

Seen from the side, the raised wings create a long, graceful silhouette across the surrounding leaves. This wider view balances the moth's saturated color with the cool greens of the rain-damp meadow and preserves the sense of discovering it low within the vegetation.
