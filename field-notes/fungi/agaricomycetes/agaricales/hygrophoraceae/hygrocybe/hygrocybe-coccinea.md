## Description
Hygrocybe coccinea, commonly known as the Scarlet Waxy Cap, is a small, brightly colored mushroom that belongs to the family Hygrophoraceae. It is characterized by its smooth, conical cap that is typically bright red in color, and a slender stem that is often paler than the cap.

For representative images and visual identification, please refer to the [Scarlet Waxy Cap](/gallery/fungi/mushrooms/scarlet-waxy-cap/) in the **Gallery** section.

## Substrate andHabitat
Hygrocybe coccinea is typically found in grassy areas, mossy grounds, and open woodlands. It prefers nutrient-poor soils and is often associated with undisturbed habitats. This species is commonly observed during the late summer to autumn months.

## Identification Features
- **Cap:** Smooth, conical, bright red, often with a slightly sticky surface when fresh. The cap may become more flattened with age.
- **Gills:** Adnate to slightly decurrent, pale yellow to cream in color, and widely spaced.
- **Stem:** Slender, smooth, and often paler than the cap, ranging from yellow to light red.
- **Spore Print:** White to pale cream.

## Ecological Notes
Hygrocybe coccinea is an indicator species for healthy, undisturbed ecosystems. It is sensitive to environmental changes and pollution, making it a valuable species for ecological monitoring. The presence of this mushroom can indicate a well-preserved habitat with low nutrient levels.
