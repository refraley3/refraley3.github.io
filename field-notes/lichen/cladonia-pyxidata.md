## *Cladonia pyxidata*
{: .taxonomy-heading}

### Family: [Cladoniaceae](/field-notes/lichen/family/cladoniaceae/)
{: .taxonomy-heading .taxonomy-last}

**Common Pixie-cup** (*Cladonia pyxidata*) is a cup-forming lichen characterized by coarse, corticate granules on its stalks and cup interiors, together with small basal scales.

## Description

- **Basal thallus:** small, rounded to elongated scales called squamules  
- **Podetia:** upright, gray to brownish cup-bearing structures  
- **Cups:** expanded tips, called scyphi, tapering toward the base  
- **Surface:** uneven, with coarse granules covered by an organized outer layer, or cortex  
- **Reproductive structures:** brown apothecia and pycnidia may occur along cup rims  

---

## Substrate and Habitat

Occurs on soil, mossy rocks, walls, and tree trunks, chiefly in relatively dry, mildly acidic to mildly base-rich habitats.

---

## Identification

**Key features for field diagnosis:**

- Coarse, corticate granules, especially within the cups  
- Absence of true powdery soredia  
- Small basal squamules  

*Cladonia chlorophaea* has more powdery or granular surfaces; poorly developed specimens can be difficult to separate. *C. pocillum* and *C. monomorpha* have similar cups but larger basal squamules. Examine both the basal thallus and cup surfaces before assigning a species name.

---

## Ecology and Notes

*Cladonia pyxidata* is variable and frequently misidentified. Its taxonomic limits require further study, so identifications should rest on the combination of characters rather than cup shape alone.
