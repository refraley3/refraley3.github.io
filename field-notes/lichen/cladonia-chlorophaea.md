## *Cladonia chlorophaea*
{: .taxonomy-heading}

### Family: Cladoniaceae
{: .taxonomy-heading .taxonomy-last}

*Cladonia chlorophaea* is a small cup-forming lichen with granular reproductive powder on its upright stalks and cup surfaces. This account concerns the species in the strict sense (*sensu stricto*), rather than the broader group of similar-looking lichens historically recorded under the same name.

## Description

*Cladonia chlorophaea* is characterized by:

- **Growth form:** small basal scales (squamules) supporting upright cup-bearing stalks (podetia)
- **Color:** greenish grey, sometimes tinged brown
- **Stalks:** usually 0.5–2.5 cm tall
- **Cups:** generally 3–6 mm across, tapering gradually into the stalk
- **Surface features:** granular soredia, sometimes mixed with small scales and corticate granules
- **Basal scales:** often small and inconspicuous

The upper stalks and cup interiors bear granular to sorediate surfaces. Brown fruiting bodies may develop along the cup margins. These characters are described in the [British Lichen Society species account](https://britishlichensociety.org.uk/resources/species-accounts/cladonia-chlorophaea-s-str).

## Substrate and Habitat

Reported substrates and settings include:

- Soil on loamy banks
- Acid grassland
- Bark and wood
- Decaying worked timber

Compared with the *Cladonia grayi* group, *C. chlorophaea* tends to occupy less strongly acidic substrates. Habitat is useful context, but does not establish an identification by itself. See the [British Lichen Society complex account](https://britishlichensociety.org.uk/resources/species-accounts/cladonia-chlorophaea-s-lat).

## Identification

**Key features for field diagnosis:**

- **Form:** gradually widening cups on upright stalks
- **Surface:** granular soredia on the upper stalks and within cups
- **Reproductive structures:** brown apothecia or pycnidia, when present
- **Chemistry:** reported reactions K−, C−, KC−, Pd+ red, UV−; fumarprotocetraric acid present

The chemistry above follows the [British Lichen Society species account](https://britishlichensociety.org.uk/resources/species-accounts/cladonia-chlorophaea-s-str).

*Cladonia fimbriata* has a finer, flour-like sorediate covering and more abruptly tapering cups. Members of the *C. grayi* group can closely resemble *C. chlorophaea*; reliable separation may require thin-layer chromatography or other specialist chemical examination. Cup shape and photographs alone may not resolve these taxa. See the [complex identification account](https://britishlichensociety.org.uk/resources/species-accounts/cladonia-chlorophaea-s-lat).

---

## Ecology and Notes

- The traditional *C. chlorophaea* complex groups lichens by similar appearance; it is not a single closely related lineage.
- *C. chlorophaea* in the strict sense is distinct from the *C. grayi* aggregate.
- Records labelled *C. chlorophaea sensu lato* refer to the broader complex and should not automatically be treated as confirmed records of this species.

These distinctions follow the [British Lichen Society treatment of the complex](https://britishlichensociety.org.uk/resources/species-accounts/cladonia-chlorophaea-s-lat).
