### [*Poecile atricapillus*](/field-notes/birds/passeriformes/paridae/poecile-atricapillus/)
{: .taxonomy-heading .taxonomy-last}

The [**Black-capped Chickadee**](/gallery/animals/birds/perching/black-capped-chickadee/) photographs collected here span several encounters and show the species in very different settings. For identification, habitat, and natural-history details, see the [*Poecile atricapillus*](/field-notes/birds/passeriformes/passerellidae/melospiza-melodia/) species page.

Taken separately, many of the photographs are familiar sparrow portraits. Together, they show how much the character of a Song Sparrow image can change when the bird's posture, breast pattern, and surrounding perch are allowed to remain part of the story.
