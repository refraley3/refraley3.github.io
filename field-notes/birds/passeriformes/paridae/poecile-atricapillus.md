## *Poecile atricapillus*
{: .taxonomy-heading}

### Family: [Parulidae](/field-notes/birds/passeriformes/parulidae/)
{: .taxonomy-heading .taxonomy-last}

[*Black-capped Chickadee](/gallery/animals/birds/perching/black-capped-chickadee/) (*Paecile atricapillus*) is a small northern songbird recognized by its black cap and throat, white cheeks, soft gray back, and pale underside. Its compact shape and inquisitive behavior make it a familiar presence in woodland edges, parks, and backyard feeding areas.
