## Side by Side

The crane on the left appears larger in the frame, while the second stands slightly behind and to the right. Their bills point in the same direction, and both hold their heads above the vegetation. The bright foreground conceals their feet, leaving the necks, crowns, and body contours as the strongest visual features.

This view documents their shared posture at one instant. It does not show a walking sequence, feeding action, or courtship display.

## Featured Observation

The red crowns and pale facial areas remain visible against the darker trees. Brown tones dominate the bodies, with gray feather areas showing through. The two necks form a repeated shape across the composition.
