This section is a working field notebook focused on the observation and identification of **butterflies and moths** through photography, comparison, and repeated field encounters.

The emphasis here is on **family-, genus-, and species-level recognition** and **diagnostic patterns**—antennae, wing shape, color placement, resting posture, flight behavior, host plants, habitat, and seasonality—rather than identification by color alone. Photographs can leave important characters hidden; uncertainty is preserved when the available evidence does not support a definitive identification.

Content in this section may include:

- Comparative notes between similar families, genera, or species  
- Field observations recorded across seasons and locations  
- Identification frameworks drawn from regional field guides and taxonomic references  
- Notes on adult behavior, larval host plants, and life stages  
- Provisional or evolving identifications

This material is intentionally separated from the Gallery.  
The Gallery presents butterflies and moths as photographic subjects; these field notes explore them as organisms and field characters.

Both perspectives inform one another.
